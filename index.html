The Castle Remembers

The castle was never empty. Beneath its stone corridors, something has been waiting.

A desperate call from Maximus draws his brother into the depths. What begins as a rescue becomes a descent through invasions, sealed chambers, blood-soaked rituals and remnants of a history that refuses to stay buried.

Find your brother. Survive the castle. Do not trust what waits beneath it.

Music From The Descent

The sound of Harvest the Cortex is part of the world itself. From suffocating dungeon ambience to the violence of an invasion, music was chosen to make every room, fight and descent feel heavier. The game features original music alongside tracks from artists who helped give this project its identity.

Special Thanks

Primal Horde

The First To Enter The Castle

You were there early, when this project was still taking shape, and your willingness to trust the vision and allow your music to become part of this world genuinely means a lot to me.

Having Primal Horde's music blasting through the castle helped define some of the game's most intense moments and gave Harvest the Cortex a piece of its musical identity.

Thank you for taking a chance on the project, for supporting something independent, and most importantly, thank you for the trust.

You'll always be part of the early history of Harvest the Cortex.

— John Stone
